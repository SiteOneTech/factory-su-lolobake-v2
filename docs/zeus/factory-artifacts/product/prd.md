## Problem Statement
LOLO's Bake needs a bilingual website that does more than look beautiful. It must generate qualified inquiries from affluent South Florida buyers who want premium artisanal desserts or celebration treats that feel safer, more thoughtful, and more trustworthy for people living with dietary restrictions or health-related needs.

The current market pain is not just “I need a cake.” It is often:
- “I need something beautiful that still feels careful.”
- “I need to trust the ingredients and process.”
- “I want a premium option without feeling excluded or medically othered.”
- “I need an easy way to ask if this is right for me or my family.”

The product must therefore position LOLO's Bake as a **lead-generating, trust-building, artisanal premium brand** for health-conscious and restriction-aware buyers — without making improper medical claims.

## Product Goal
Create a bilingual ES/EN website and strategy package that:
1. generates more qualified leads via WhatsApp `+1 (786) 296-6190` and inquiry form;
2. positions LOLO's Bake as an artisanal, premium, health-conscious brand for restricted-diet and wellness-aware buyers in South Florida;
3. communicates trust, indulgence, and care without sounding clinical;
4. clarifies what messaging, positioning, and conversion paths should scale after launch.

## Primary Success Thesis
**Primary business objective:** lead generation.

The MVP succeeds if it increases:
- qualified inquiries;
- WhatsApp starts;
- form submissions;
- buyer confidence;
- clarity of brand positioning in the niche.

## Value Hypothesis
If LOLO's Bake communicates that customers with dietary restrictions or health-related food concerns can still enjoy premium, beautiful, artisanal treats with confidence, then affluent South Florida buyers will be more likely to inquire, trust the brand, and convert into orders.

## Target Users

### Primary buyer
Affluent South Florida adults or families seeking premium desserts/treats aligned with dietary restrictions, ingredient sensitivity, or health-conscious preferences.

### Secondary buyer
Caregivers, partners, event planners, or family members buying for someone with diabetes-adjacent concerns, gluten-free/celiac needs, anti-inflammatory preferences, or similar restriction-aware requirements.

### Tertiary buyer
Bilingual South Florida shoppers who value artisanal quality, wellness alignment, premium aesthetics, and easy direct communication.

## Positioning Recommendation
### Recommended lane
**Artisanal, premium, bilingual dessert brand for health-conscious and restriction-aware South Florida buyers.**

### Brand tension to solve
The brand must sit in the middle of four forces:
- premium
- artesanal
- saludable / mindful
- emotionally warm

It must avoid becoming either:
- a generic cake boutique, or
- a cold clinical nutrition product.

### Working positioning thesis
LOLO's Bake helps people celebrate, gift, and indulge beautifully **without feeling excluded by dietary restrictions or forced into joyless alternatives**.

# Acceptance Criteria — LOLO's Bake v2

## Product Acceptance Criteria

### AC1 — Lead generation is primary
- The site is explicitly designed to generate qualified inquiries and new customers.
- CTA hierarchy supports visit → inquiry rather than passive browsing.

### AC2 — Niche fit is clear
- The site clearly explains who LOLO's Bake is for, including buyers with dietary restrictions, ingredient concerns, or health-conscious preferences.
- This is done without improper medical claims.

### AC3 — Bilingual localized experience
- ES/EN content is localized, intentional, and usable.
- The bilingual experience improves trust and conversion.

### AC4 — Premium artisanal positioning
- The copy and layout present LOLO's Bake as premium, artisanal, warm, and trustworthy.
- The experience does not read primarily as a generic celebration cake boutique.

### AC5 — Trust modules exist
- The MVP includes sections such as “para quién es / who it’s for,” care principles, or FAQs that reduce restriction-related trust friction.

### AC6 — Gallery and proof support conversion
- Visual proof exists, but supports the trust and inquiry narrative rather than replacing it.

### AC7 — Inquiry-first funnel works
- WhatsApp and form inquiry paths are functional and clear.
- The site explains how to begin the conversation.

### AC8 — Mobile-first and accessible
- Key sections and CTAs are usable on mobile.
- The site remains fast and trust-building.

### AC9 — SEO is niche-aware
- Basic SEO reflects health-conscious / artisanal / South Florida intent themes without unsafe claims.

### AC10 — Strategy output completeness
- The strategic package includes branding, positioning, lead generation, affluent-neighborhood targeting guidance, tone of voice, and niche strategy.

## Non-Goals
1. Ecommerce or checkout.
2. Admin or backoffice tooling.
3. Full medical/nutrition advisory content.
4. Unsupported clinical claims.
5. Generic celebration-first positioning as the primary business thesis.
